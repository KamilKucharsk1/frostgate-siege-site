Frostgate Siege

PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 21 November 2026

Frostgate Siege does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The game has no analytics, no advertising, no user accounts and makes no network connections of any kind. It runs entirely offline.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress — which stages you have cleared, how many stars you earned, and which permanent upgrades you bought in the War Council — and your settings: sound, music and vibration on or off. This is the only information the game writes anywhere.

It is stored on your device only, using the operating system's standard storage for app settings. It is never sent anywhere and the developer has no access to it.

If you have device backups enabled, your saved progress may be included in those backups along with the rest of your app data. That backup is handled by Apple, under Apple's own privacy terms, not by this game.

Deleting the game deletes the saved progress with it.

What the game does not do

  • No personal data is collected — no name, e-mail, phone number or address.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No user accounts, sign-in or social features.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health data.
  • No network requests. All game content ships inside the app and works with the device offline or in airplane mode.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services. No data is shared with anyone, because none is gathered.

The game is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple collects its own information about downloads and purchases under Apple's Privacy Policy. That is outside the developer's control and independent of this game.

Children

The game is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included. It contains no advertising, no purchases and no way to communicate with other people.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever collects data — for example if advertising is added — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The current version of the game collects nothing.
