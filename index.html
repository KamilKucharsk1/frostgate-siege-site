About the game

Frostgate Siege is a tower defense game for iPhone. Build and upgrade towers along the road, choose a specialisation for each, cast spells and hold the pass across a 25-stage campaign. Stars earned in battle buy permanent upgrades in the War Council.

Common questions

  • Does the game need the internet? No. It works fully offline, including in airplane mode.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the game deletes the progress with it.
  • How do I start over? Main menu → Reset progress.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.
